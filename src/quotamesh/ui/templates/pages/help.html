What does QuotaMesh do?

QuotaMesh sends your application's Chat Completions requests through the provider/model order saved in your profile. Your app uses one local endpoint; you decide which access it can use and whether paid fallback is allowed.

  1. Your client sends a request

    The local gateway checks authorization and resolves an alias such as qm/default.

  2. Your saved profile defines the order

    A target is an exact provider, model, and key or key pool. Draft edits do not affect requests until saved.

  3. The gateway checks eligibility

    Disabled keys, expired trials, shared cooldowns, and blocked paid access are skipped. A dry run performs these checks without contacting a provider.

  4. An eligible target receives your request

    Retryable failures can move to the next configured target before a response starts. After streaming starts, an interruption stays on that provider.

  5. You inspect the outcome

    Activity records attempt order, timing, usage, and cost evidence. Prompts and responses are never saved in history.

What the numbers mean

You entered
Plan, expiry, starting dollar credit, grouping, and optional model prices.
Provider reported
Usage or cost returned by the provider for a request. A dated rate snapshot can become stale.
Gateway observed / estimated
Requests through this gateway and estimates based on reported tokens and your prices. Other traffic is invisible.
Unknown
No reliable evidence. Unknown quota, usage, or cost must not be treated as zero.
Shared quota group
Keys belonging to one provider account/project share limits. Adding keys does not multiply capacity.
Project profile
One app's ordered targets and permissions. Clients use its immutable qm/<slug> alias.

Privacy and practical limits

This gateway runs on loopback in one process. Directly saved secrets are plaintext in local SQLite; environment references avoid storing a key. No telemetry or background provider checks run.

Paid and unknown-plan access require permission in the saved profile. Local caps compare observed spend before selection; requests already in flight can exceed them. They are not provider billing limits.

Chat Completions is supported. Model listing does not prove generation permissions or remaining quota. QuotaMesh does not create capacity or bypass provider limits.

If your session expires after restarting the gateway, reopen the one-time dashboard link printed by quotamesh start.
